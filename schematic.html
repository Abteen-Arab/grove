Balance makes room for more life.

04 / What the reference teaches us

a.

Give effort a visible home

Forest puts a growing tree at the center. Grove expands that stage into a landscape, where each habit supports a different kind of life.

b.

Keep the next action obvious

A large timer and one clear control carry the tracking flow. Goals and sources stay in sheets, close to their context.

c.

Keep the surface quiet

Green canvases, warm earth, cream surfaces, and short labels preserve the calm of the classic reference.

d.

Make balance visible

One strong habit cannot fill every habitat. Varied progress brings varied wildlife, while the guide preserves each discovery.

Reference date: the closest visually verified classic screens are from September 2022. A later walkthrough documents Forest through November 2023, version 4.71.0. They guide this adaptation without claiming an exact copy of every 2023 screen.
