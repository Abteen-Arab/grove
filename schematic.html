02 / What the reference teaches us

a.

Make the reward the focus

Forest’s classic tree stage puts the growing tree at the center. Grove places the habitat above the numbers, so effort has a visible destination.

b.

Keep the main action obvious

A clear timer and planting control make Forest’s core flow easy to follow. Grove keeps that hierarchy and moves configuration into sheets.

c.

Use a calm, natural palette

The dated reference shows a green field, pale tree stage, and brighter timer ring. Grove adapts these roles into green canvases, cream surfaces, and soft accents.

d.

Let small efforts accumulate

Forest turns focus sessions into an accumulating forest. Grove extends growth to three habit goals, while keeping each habit visible.

Reference date: the closest visually verified classic screens are from September 2022. A later walkthrough documents Forest through November 2023, version 4.71.0. Together they guide this adaptation; they do not verify every 2023 screen. Current official pages may show later artwork and features.

03 / Growth with clear rules

One goal → one tree

Each habit can earn one tree per day. Screen time earns its tree after the day is finished within the chosen limit.

One session → one tree

Complete the countdown to grow a tree. Quick demo mode makes one real second equal one focus minute.

Three goals → a visitor

A balanced day welcomes one visitor. The starting eight trees and two visitors are sample history.

Challenge points: equal-weight progress toward personal goals, capped at 100% per habit. Compare the last seven days with the previous seven. Scores show the change in percentage points; peers are samples. This is a game mechanic, not a health score.
